# Primary mathematical sources: original publications and full texts

**Source classes**

- **Original facsimile:** scanned pages from a contemporary journal or original book; strongest evidence for typography, figures, wording, and pagination.
- **Historical collected edition:** a later printed edition of the original author's work; identify editorial changes.
- **Modern transcription:** full primary text reset in modern type, **not** a photograph of the original.
- **Translation:** useful for reading, but wording must be checked against the original if an historical claim depends on it.

Original publication and modern scan/transcription are *not* the same thing. Links below favor libraries, learned societies, and publicly inspectable scans.

## Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz (1646–1716)

### 1684 — *Nova methodus pro maximis et minimis, itemque tangentibus…*

- **Original:** *Acta Eruditorum*, October 1684, pp. 467–473. Latin.
- **Original-volume scan:** [*Acta Eruditorum*, 1684 (Internet Archive PDF)](https://archive.org/download/s1id13206500/s1id13206500.pdf).
- **Context and reproduced pages:** [Mathematical Association of America: Leibniz's differential-calculus paper](https://old.maa.org/press/periodicals/convergence/mathematical-treasure-leibnizs-papers-on-calculus-differential-calculus).
- **Read for:** the published differential calculus; identify precisely the relation between calculation, tangents, curves, and what Leibniz then called a construction.

### 1686 — *De geometria recondita et analysi indivisibilium atque infinitorum*

- **Original:** *Acta Eruditorum*, June 1686, pp. 292–300. Latin.
- **Original-volume scan:** [*Acta Eruditorum*, 1686 (Internet Archive PDF)](https://archive.org/download/s1id13206520/s1id13206520.pdf).
- **Bibliographic confirmation:** [Smithsonian Libraries: separately catalogued offprint](https://www.si.edu/object/siris_sil_95797).
- **Page images and notes:** [Mathematical Association of America](https://old.maa.org/press/periodicals/convergence/mathematical-treasure-leibnizs-papers-on-calculus-integral-calculus).
- **Read for:** integration and quadrature in Leibniz's own notation; do not attribute later terminology verbatim to the original.

### 1691 — two catenary papers, with a direct logarithm connection

1. *De linea in quam flexile se pondere proprio curvat, ejusque usu insigni ad inveniendas quotcunque medias proportionales et logarithmos*; *Acta Eruditorum*, June 1691, pp. 277–281. Latin.
2. *De solutionibus problematis catenarii vel funicularis in Actis Junii an. 1691, aliisque a Dn. Jac. Bernoullio propositis*; *Acta Eruditorum*, September 1691, pp. 435–439. Latin.

**Actual primary-source scan:** [*Acta Eruditorum* 1691, complete original volume (636-page PDF)](https://archive.org/download/s1id13206570/s1id13206570.pdf), [Wikimedia Commons catalog and scan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Acta_eruditorum._1691_(IA_s1id13206570).pdf). Bibliographic corroboration for the second item: [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/la-rivoluzione-scientifica-fonti_%28Storia-della-Scienza%29/).

- **Modern English translation (copyrighted, not original facsimile):** [Pierre Beaudry, *G. W. Leibniz: Two Papers on the Catenary Curve and Logarithmic Curve*, *Fidelio*, Spring 2001](https://archive.schillerinstitute.com/fid_97-01/011_catenary.html).
- **Further comparison:** [2019 scholarly study of Leibniz's calculations and the constant e](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.hm.2019.06.001).
- **Read for:** the geometry of a hanging chain, relation of logarithmic and catenary curves, rectification, tangency, the exponential/logarithmic construction. Distinguish Leibniz's arguments from the **modern** equation `y = a cosh(x/a)`, which is a useful reconstruction, not his printed notation.

### 1691 — Leibniz's letters to von Bodenhausen and *Analysis problematis catenarii* (primary manuscripts)

The June 1691 printed construction is **not** the full historical derivation. Leibniz discusses its hidden numerical normalization and his analysis in **contemporaneous unpublished correspondence**:

- **12/22 June 1691**, Leibniz to Rudolf Christian von Bodenhausen, **No. 24, p. 118 ff.**, supplying the crucial proportions **0.3678794 : 1 : 2.7182818**, equivalent to the reciprocal/exponential numbers now called `e⁻¹ : 1 : e`.
- **10/20 August 1691**, Leibniz to von Bodenhausen, **No. 33, pp. 143–153**.
- *Analysis problematis catenarii*, accompanying Latin mathematical derivation, **No. 34, pp. 154–155**.

These are printed in the scholarly **Leibniz-Akademie-Ausgabe**, Series III, vol. 5 (edited H.-J. Heß and James G. O'Hara; 2003). **Edition of the original author's letters**, not an *Acta Eruditorum* article or seventeenth-century facsimile. [Edition landing page (Leibniz Archive, rights CC BY-NC 4.0)](https://www.gwlb.de/leibniz/digitale-ressourcen/repositorium-des-leibniz-archivs/laa-bd-iii-5); [downloadable part A PDF](https://rep.adw-goe.de/bitstream/handle/11858/2441/III5A.pdf).

- **Historical corroboration:** Michael Raugh and Siegmund Probst, [“The Leibniz catenary and approximation of e — an analysis of his unpublished calculations”](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0315086018301290), *Historia Mathematica* **49** (2019), pp. 1–19. They examine the surviving original arithmetic sheets and identify the omitted constant in the geometric construction.
- **Mathematical reading / normalization audit:** [Leibniz 1691 primary-source comparison](leibniz-catenary-primary-audit.md).

### Other Leibniz material to track

- *Considérations sur la différence entre l'analyse ordinaire et le nouveau calcul des transcendantes* (1694): check collected works / original publication before assigning an unverified journal citation.
- *Quadratura arithmetica circuli, ellipseos et hyperbolae* (1675–76 manuscript): manuscript, **not** a published 1670s paper.
- *De la chainette ou solution d'un problème fameux…* (*Journal des sçavans*, March 1692, pp. 147–153), a separate French publication; do not substitute this for the June and September 1691 Latin papers.

## Roger Cotes (1682–1716)

### 1714 — *Logometria*

- **Original:** Roger Cotes, *Logometria*, *Philosophical Transactions* 29 (1714–1716), pp. 5–45. Latin.
- **DOI:** [10.1098/rstl.1714.0002](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstl.1714.0002).
- **Original-paper facsimile:** [Wikimedia Commons, 41-page PDF](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Logometria_Auctore_Rogero_Cotes,_Trin._Coll._Cantab._Soc._Astr._%26_Ph._Exp._Professore_Plumiano,_%26_R._S._S._(IA_paper-doi-10_1098_rstl_1714_0002).pdf).
- **Read for:** logarithmic measurement, angles, series, circular/hyperbolic relationships. Keep the geometry and modern complex-logarithm interpretation separate.

### 1722 — *Harmonia mensurarum sive analysis et synthesis per rationum et angulorum mensuras promotae*

- **Original book:** posthumously issued 1722; edited and augmented by Robert Smith. Latin.
- **Actual first-edition facsimile:** [ETH-Bibliothek / e-rara digital scan](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/titleinfo/1255698), [persistent DOI 10.3931/e-rara-4027](https://doi.org/10.3931/e-rara-4027). Full 53.54 MB PDF offered on landing page; public-domain mark.
- **Read for:** logarithms and angle measures as linked mathematical structures. Distinguish text by Cotes from editorial material by Smith.

## Carl Friedrich Gauss (1777–1855)

### 1799 — *Demonstratio nova theorematis…*

- **Original:** Gauss's dissertation on factorization of real polynomials (fundamental theorem of algebra). Latin.
- **Digitized original:** [Humboldt-Universität library scan](https://edoc.hu-berlin.de/items/0d7bf8a1-c3a8-408f-bc0d-4e068559f3e2), [DOI 10.18452/80](https://doi.org/10.18452/80).
- **Read for:** Gauss's argument, its geometrical assumptions and what is actually established, as distinct from later accounts.

### 1809 — *Theoria motus corporum coelestium in sectionibus conicis solem ambientium*

- **Original book:** Hamburg, 1809. Latin.
- **First-edition digitization:** [ETH-Bibliothek / e-rara](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/titleinfo/142787), [DOI 10.3931/e-rara-522](https://doi.org/10.3931/e-rara-522).
- **Historical Ceres context:** [MacTutor: search for the missing planet](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/HistTopics/Missing_Planet/) and [Gauss's preface to the *Theoria motus*](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Extras/Gauss_preface/).
- **Read for:** the published orbital-determination theory; compare its published procedure with what Gauss specifically said about his earlier Ceres calculation. **Piazzi discovered Ceres in 1801; Gauss reconstructed its orbit.**

## Bernhard Riemann (1826–1866)

[David R. Wilkins's edition of the complete selected mathematical papers](https://www.emis.de/classics/Riemann/) is a useful full-text collection. **Wilkins reset the texts**; they are modern transcriptions based on historical editions, not contemporary journal scans. For the 1857 Abelian paper an original-edition scan is also linked below.

### 1851 — *Grundlagen für eine allgemeine Theorie der Functionen einer veränderlichen complexen Grösse*

- **Original:** Göttingen inaugural dissertation, 1851. German.
- **Full text, modern transcription:** [Wilkins PDF](https://www.emis.de/classics/Riemann/Grund.pdf).
- **Read for:** complex variable, analytic continuation, multivalued functions, and foundations of surfaces.

### 1857 — *Beiträge zur Theorie der durch die Gauss'sche Reihe F(α, β, γ, x) darstellbaren Functionen*

- **Original:** *Abhandlungen der Königlichen Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften zu Göttingen* 7 (1857), pp. 3–32. German.
- **Full text, modern transcription:** [Wilkins PDF](https://www.emis.de/classics/Riemann/PFunct.pdf).
- **Read for:** Gauss's hypergeometric equation/series, singularities and continuation, before relying on Director Part 64.

### 1857 — *Theorie der Abel'schen Functionen*

- **Original:** *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik* 54 (1857), pp. 101–155 **as stated in the Wilkins transcription**. Check original pagination if making page-precise quotations; external catalogs differ.
- **Contemporary separate-edition facsimile:** [ETH-Bibliothek / e-rara, Berlin 1857](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/titleinfo/13599822), [DOI 10.3931/e-rara-49484](https://doi.org/10.3931/e-rara-49484) (public-domain, full 15.35 MB download).
- **Full text, modern transcription:** [Wilkins PDF](https://www.emis.de/classics/Riemann/AbelFn.pdf).
- **Especially important primary passage:** beginning of §1: Riemann announces investigations of “Transcendenten”; §§1–2 discuss analytic continuation, `log(z − a)`, its `2πi` increment on circling `a`, branching, sheets, and topology; §3 discusses determination of transcendental functions and Dirichlet's principle.
- **Read for:** Riemann's own definitions/operations, not retroactively imposed accounts of “transcendental.”

### 1866 — *Ueber das Verschwinden der Theta-Functionen*

- **Original:** *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik* 65 (1866), pp. 161–172.
- **Full text, modern transcription:** [Wilkins PDF](https://www.emis.de/classics/Riemann/Theta.pdf) (**PDF filename should be checked against the Wilkins index before automated retrieval**).
- **Read for:** theta functions and Abelian analysis.

### Lectured in 1854, published posthumously in 1868

- *Ueber die Hypothesen, welche der Geometrie zu Grunde liegen* — [German full text](https://www.emis.de/classics/Riemann/Geom.pdf), [W. K. Clifford's 1873 English translation](https://www.emis.de/classics/Riemann/WKCGeom.pdf). Keep the lecture and publication years separate.
- *Ueber die Darstellbarkeit einer Function durch eine trigonometrische Reihe* — [German full text](https://www.emis.de/classics/Riemann/Trig.pdf) (**verify filename before scripted download**).

## Emmy Noether (1882–1935)

### 1918 — *Invariante Variationsprobleme*

- **Original:** *Nachrichten von der Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften zu Göttingen, Mathematisch-Physikalische Klasse*, 1918, pp. 235–257. German.
- **Library record:** [EuDML](https://eudml.org/doc/59024); title, author, year, and pages checked against the record.
- **Original-paper facsimile:** [23-page DjVu scan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:InvarianteVariationsprobleme.djvu), with page images accessible from the catalog.
- **Modern German transcription:** [Wikisource](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Invariante_Variationsprobleme); distinguish reset text from photographed pages.
- **Account-derived route:** [the user's 2023-03-02 post](https://x.com/isomorphisms/status/1631278573674700802), reposted by @for_gP3, points to Chris Quigg's [*Colloquium: A Century of Noether's Theorem*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.01989), a 2019 history based on a 2018 colloquium. Quigg's paper is a later account, not Noether's original publication.
- **Read for:** the two different results for finite-parameter and arbitrary-function transformation groups. Record the assumptions and variation problem before applying the results to a mechanical example. Full mathematical comparison remains pending.

## How to work through the original texts

1. Record the original passage and printed page (for *Acta Eruditorum*, journal pagination differs from PDF image numbers).
2. Copy its diagram separately and state any normalization used when putting it into modern notation.
3. Reconstruct the mathematical claim, calculate or draw a concrete example, and name assumptions.
4. Only then compare it with Director, Tennenbaum, the LYM materials, or later historiography.
5. Keep a `discrepancies/` record if the secondary account differs from the actual paper.
